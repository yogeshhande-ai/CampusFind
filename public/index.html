ABOUT CAMPUSFIND

Making campus life a little easier.

CampusFind is a digital Lost & Found Portal designed to help students quickly report, search and reconnect with their lost belongings.

Instead of depending only on notice boards or messages, students can use one centralized platform to find recently reported items.
